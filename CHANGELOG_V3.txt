MedFed Group 28 – Multi-Disease Research V3

Added:
- Five research dashboard modules: Pneumonia, CT organ classification, breast ultrasound benign/malignant benchmark, Diabetes CSV, and Blood-test CSV.
- Reusable image CNN and tabular MLP model factories.
- Generic in-process FedAvg and FedProx training engine.
- IID and Dirichlet non-IID client partitioning.
- CSV ingestion with stratified train/validation/test splits; preprocessing is fitted on training data only.
- Centralised and local-only baselines; class-wise metrics; JSON/CSV/model downloads.
- Explicit task-scope, privacy and clinical-use limitations.
- Original V2 pneumonia code retained for reference/backward compatibility.

Important:
- The dashboard's current federated simulator is in-process and single-machine. It is not a distributed Flower deployment.
- Flower adapter/strategy examples from V2 are retained but have not been promoted as a complete production Flower app.
- No formal differential privacy or secure aggregation is implemented.
- Diabetes and blood-test tasks require user-supplied documented labelled CSV files.
- BreastMNIST is an ultrasound benchmark; OrganAMNIST is CT organ classification; neither should be described as a general cancer/CT diagnostic system.
- This is a research prototype, not a clinically validated tool.
